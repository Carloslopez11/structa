Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Structa 3D before starting your free trial.

How accurate are the AI takeoffs and material calculations?
Structa 3D matches standard industry architectural scaling (1/4" = 1'-0" or metric). Our computer vision models detect wall boundaries with up to 99.4% precision. You always retain complete control: you can manually adjust any measurement, edit wall lengths, and change material unit costs before publishing the quote.
Can I use it with photos taken on my phone, or do I need CAD/DWG files?
Both! You can upload crisp vector PDFs, blueprints from architects, or simply snap a photo of a crumpled paper drawing on the hood of your truck. Structa's perspective-correction engine automatically squares and scales the drawing.
How does the 3D client render help me win more bids?
90% of homeowners and commercial clients cannot read 2D technical drawings. When competitors hand them a black & white Excel spreadsheet with rows of numbers, and you hand them a photorealistic 3D rendering with an itemized quote, you win the contract on emotion and trust immediately.
Can I customize material prices for my specific city or supplier?
Yes. Structa comes pre-loaded with average US and regional contractor price books for lumber, drywall, paint, tile, and labor. You can customize any unit price in your settings to match your local supply house discount in 1 click.
Is there a contract or commitment? Can I cancel anytime?
Zero commitment. You can cancel your subscription anytime with 1 click directly inside your account settings. There are no cancellation fees or lock-in contracts.
